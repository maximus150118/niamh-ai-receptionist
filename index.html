How it works

Three simple steps

  1. A caller rings your number

    Evenings, weekends or while you're out on a viewing, Niamh picks up and tells the caller they're speaking to an AI assistant.

  2. Niamh qualifies the enquiry

    She asks whether they want to buy, sell or let, the area, budget and timing, and takes their contact details.

  3. You get the lead straight away

    The details are emailed to your agency right after the call, or sent to your Google Sheet or CRM.
